NIKKE Overload Reroll Advisor Advanced · whole-unit planner

Set unit-wide requirements (e.g. Elemental ×4 · ATK ×4 · Ammo ×2 = a "10/12" build) and the tool works out one next move across all four pieces: what to chase, when to lock, and when a piece is done at 2 lines because a quota is covered elsewhere. The per-piece chart logic matches the Simple tab. Saved separately from the Simple tab's data.

1 · What does this unit need across all four pieces?

For each effect, set how many of the 4 pieces need it. Elemental & ATK are usually ×4 (every piece); Max Ammo often only ×2 or ×3 — that's the "10/12" / "11/12" shorthand. Passable = keep as a bonus if it shows up, never aimed for. Don't pre-plan which pieces get the ×2/×3 effects — the tool assigns them to whichever pieces roll them first (that's the cheap way), and marks other pieces done at 2 lines once a quota is covered.

2 · Enter the gear pieces

Enter what the game currently shows on all four pieces before spending anything — the plan optimizes with full information. Tick Lock for lines you've locked with a Custom Module.

3 · Next move — all four pieces considered

One recommendation, chosen across the whole unit: the cheapest action that makes progress toward the build. Steps for other pieces wait their turn.

4 · Unit totals — all four pieces combined

Every rolled effect summed across Head / Arm / Body / Leg. Per the guide, a roll at or above its minimum counts as 1×, below it as 0.5× (Charge Damage / Crit / DEF always 1×). Use this for stacked requirements like "120% Max Ammo total".

Custom (temporary) locks hold a line for one reroll and save modules — great for batching value resets. Unlocking never refunds modules.

The two reroll buttons & key definitions
  • Change Effects — rerolls the effect types on every unlocked line (values change too). Use this to fish for the stats you want.
  • Reset Attributes — rerolls only the value % of existing effects on unlocked lines; types stay, empty lines stay empty. Use this to push a good stat above its minimum.
  • Blue line = Tier 12–14 (rare, high value). Black line = Tier 15 (highest, ~1% — basically never reroll these away).
  • Lock a good line before Change-Effects fishing so you don't lose it. Locking Line 1 is discouraged unless it's Blue/Black, because Line 1 always rolls something anyway — you'd rather keep it free and fill it last.

both on Line 2+3 Reset to min → Lock both Line 2 & 3 → Change Effects for the last on Line 1 → reset → done. (least risk)
one on Line 1, Blue/Black Lock Line 1 → Lock the other → Change Effects for the last on the remaining line. (highest risk)
one on Line 1, not Blue/Black Treat the Line 1 copy as expendable churn: reset & lock the Line 2/3 one, then Change Effects until the remaining targets land on the free lines.
Three targets present:
done Reset attributes so each meets its minimum — finished.
